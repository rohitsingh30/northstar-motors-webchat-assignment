For the test-drive flow, verify:
- Select a vehicle.
- Select a date and time.
- Enter invalid contact details.
- Correct the details.
- Review the booking.
- Cancel from the top-right close button.
- Confirm the booking.
- Reopen and close “View booked test drive.”
