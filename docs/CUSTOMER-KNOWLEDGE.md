## Finance terminology

### PCH

PCH means Personal Contract Hire. It is a vehicle lease: the customer normally pays an initial
rental and fixed monthly rentals for an agreed term and mileage, then returns the vehicle. The
customer does not own the vehicle. Terms and availability vary by published offer, and finance is
subject to status.

### PCP

PCP means Personal Contract Purchase. It normally uses a deposit and fixed monthly payments, with
an optional final payment if the customer wants to keep the vehicle. Terms vary by published offer,
and finance is subject to status.

## Part-exchange estimates

Northstar's online part-exchange estimate uses the vehicle registration, current mileage, and
customer-selected condition to produce an indicative valuation range. It is not a guaranteed
purchase price and remains subject to physical inspection, provenance checks, and current market
conditions.
